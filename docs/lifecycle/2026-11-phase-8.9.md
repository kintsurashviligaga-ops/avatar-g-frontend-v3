## 2. Safety identifier: draft PR #371 (flag off)

- Google puts `safety_identifier` **inside `labels`** on Gemini `generateContent` / `streamGenerateContent` and on Veo `predictLongRunning`.
- The value must be 1–63 characters of `[a-z0-9_-]`, with no PII.
- PR #371 sends `u` + 40 hex characters of HMAC-SHA256(salt, account id).
- It is on only when `SAFETY_IDENTIFIER=1` and `SAFETY_IDENTIFIER_SALT` are both set.

Sources:
- https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/abuse-monitoring
- https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/capabilities/add-labels-to-api-calls
